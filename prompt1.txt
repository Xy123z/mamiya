# Animation Specification: Ethereal Text Disintegration

## Objective

Implement a cinematic text-dissolution animation for the following exact sentence:

**“Those who want to know themselves come here.”**

The objective is to make this sentence gradually disintegrate into extremely fine, luminous dust particles, creating a subtle, poetic and atmospheric visual experience.


Specific requirements:

- Text: Pure or slightly warm white (#F5F5F5).
- Typography: Elegant serif typeface, preferably Georgia or a visually similar literary serif font.
- Font weight: Regular.
- Alignment: Perfectly centred horizontally and vertically within its container.
- Text layout: A single uninterrupted line on desktop.
- Preserve generous negative space around the text.


## 2. Disintegration Concept

After the initial text has remained visible until i press the scroll button.

The letters should appear to lose their physical structure, breaking apart into hundreds or thousands of extremely fine, glowing particles.

The transformation must originate from the actual shape of the rendered typography.

Do not simply fade out the original text and display an unrelated particle animation.

Instead, make the individual particles emerge from the visible pixels of the original letters, preserving the illusion that the text itself is physically dissolving.

## 3. Particle Appearance

The particles should have the following characteristics:

- Extremely small, resembling fine floating dust.
- Primarily warm white
- Variable sizes, with most particles being barely visible.
- Soft, restrained luminous glow.
- Slightly varying brightness and opacity.
- No large circular particles, sparks, flames or explosive visual effects.

The particles should initially inherit the colour of the original white typography before gradually developing a faint warm glow.

The overall appearance should resemble illuminated dust floating in darkness.

## 4. Particle Movement

The movement should be delicate, organic and atmospheric.

As the text disintegrates:

- Particles should gradually detach from their original positions.
- Introduce subtle horizontal and vertical drift.
- Use smooth turbulence or noise-based movement.
- Give individual particles slightly different velocities and lifetimes.
- Allow some particles to float gently upwards.
- Allow others to drift sideways or slightly downwards.
- Avoid uniform movement and mechanical patterns.

The particles should travel relatively short distances before gradually fading into the darkness.

Importantly, the effect should feel like the sentence is evaporating rather than exploding.

## 5. Progressive Dissolution

The entire sentence must not disappear simultaneously.

Implement a progressive disintegration effect.

The dissolution should begin subtly around the edges of individual letters and gradually spread through their interiors.

Different portions of the sentence should disintegrate at slightly different moments, creating a naturally irregular appearance.

The original text should gradually become less readable as more particles detach.

Eventually, the entire sentence should disappear, leaving only the floating particles.

## 7. Technical Implementation

Use the existing React + Vite architecture.

Recommended technologies:

- React functional component.
- HTML Canvas 2D API.
- Offscreen canvas for rendering the original text.
- `getImageData()` for extracting visible typography pixels.
- JavaScript particle system.
- `requestAnimationFrame()` for animation.

Generate particles directly from the original text's visible pixels.

Each particle should maintain its own:

- Position.
- Velocity.
- Size.
- Opacity.
- Lifetime.
- Movement variation.

Use subtle canvas glow effects and, where appropriate, additive blending.

Ensure that the animation is responsive and performs efficiently on both desktop and mobile devices.

## 8. Important Design Constraints

The original typography is an essential part of the visual identity.

Do not:

- Change the sentence or its punctuation.
- Replace the serif typography with a modern sans-serif font.
- Introduce colourful particles.
- Make the particles excessively bright.
- Use an explosive disintegration effect.
- Add unnecessary background effects.
- Make the animation excessively fast.
- Distort the original text before the dissolution begins.

## 9. Desired Final Experience

The intended sequence is:

it slowly fades in after the programmed delay time

it remains perfectly still until I press scroll button.

after pressing the button, almost imperceptibly its letters begin releasing tiny glowing particles.

The particles gently detach, drift through the surrounding darkness and gradually disappear.

Eventually, nothing remains, and the next phase of the timeline appears

The entire experience should feel quiet, mysterious, introspective and cinematic.

**The guiding artistic idea: A sentence slowly surrendering its physical form, until even its final traces disappear into a name**
